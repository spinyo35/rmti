## Description

**Project:** Football fans don't all watch the same way. One wants the drama, one wants the numbers, one just wants to know who scored — and right now, every broadcaster gives them all the same feed. RTMI (Real-time Match Intelligence) fixes that. As the match unfolds, a team of AI agents joins you in the stands: a commentator who talks the way you like to be talked to, an analyst desk where tactics, stats and storyline experts debate live and agree on a verdict, and a chat you can just ask — "Why are Eagles struggling?" — and get an answer you can check, with the actual events it's based on. Half-time, you get a highlight reel picked just for you. Switch persona once (Casual, Stats Nerd, Kid, Tactician) and the whole broadcast changes with you. And nothing the AI says is a black box — every comment, every verdict, every highlight comes with its receipts, so you can see exactly why it said what it said.

**Goals:**
- Build something that actually works, end to end, and put it live on the web for anyone to try.
- Make every judge criteria count: real-time, agentic AI, explainable, personal, and demoable in 3 memorable minutes.
- Keep AI honest and the demo unbreakable: real event citations, honest confidence scores, and sensible fallbacks when the model has a bad day.
